## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

Say it once. Cue remembers the rest.

Cue is a planner that does the remembering for you. Type a plan the way you
would say it — "call mom tonight at 9", "submit ITR before 31 July", "kal subah
8 baje doodh lena" — and it becomes a reminder at the right time. When a line
is too vague to plan, Cue asks one short question instead of guessing, with
your free times as the answers.

FROM YOUR EMAIL, ON ITS OWN
Connect Gmail or Outlook with read-only access and Cue finds what matters:
• Train and flight tickets, hotel stays, shows and appointments — with a
  reminder when to leave, and web check-in for flights.
• Parcels from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and more — a reminder the morning
  they arrive, and the cash to keep ready.
• Bills, credit card dues, insurance renewals, PUC, FASTag and challans —
  before they are due, not after.
Cue never sends, deletes or changes an email, and keeps no copy of them.

TRIPS, END TO END
The flight out, the hotel and the flight home become one trip, day by day,
with when to leave for each journey, a packing list made for that trip, and
the things you saved "for when I'm in Goa".

WORK AND PERSONAL, ON ONE DAY
Cue tells work from personal on its own. During work hours, personal nags
wait for the evening; after hours, work ones wait for the morning. A time you
chose always rings.

A BETTER PLAN, OFFERED
A far-off deadline gets a head start. An interview gets an hour to prepare.
A birthday gets a gift reminder the evening before. A meeting on a day you
are away gets a warning, and a move to the day you are back.

AND THE REST OF A BUSY LIFE
• A morning brief: your day in one place.
• Repeating plans, in plain words: "gym every mon wed fri 7am".
• Expenses, money owed to you, and job, hiring or sales trackers.
• Speak a plan, or share a message into Cue from any app.

Free to use. Your data is yours: delete your account, and everything in it,
from Settings at any time.
